Three commitments

Embedded with your team

We work inside your tools, channels and rhythms — the way a forward-deployed engineer would — so the agent fits how work actually happens.

Production-grade by default

Error handling, retries, approvals and logging from day one. Built to run unattended, observable when it matters.

You own everything

Workflows, code, prompts and documentation live in your accounts. No black boxes, no lock-in.

Principles

How we make decisions.

  1. 01

    Start with the process, not the model

    The best agent is the one that removes the most tedious hours. We pick the technology after we understand the work.

  2. 02

    Prove it on real data

    Demos on tidy sample data hide the hard parts. We test on your real cases from the start.

  3. 03

    People approve what matters

    Agents do the legwork. Decisions with real consequences stay with your team.

  4. 04

    Simple where it counts

    Proven tools, clear flows and fewer moving parts — so it keeps working after we've gone.

  5. 05

    Leave you stronger

    Documentation, handover and training, so your team understands what's running and why.
