EngineeringFlorida, USA

AI permitting system for a Florida engineering firm

  • Claude
  • Python
  • Vision models
  • RAG
  • Supabase
  • CAD / DXF
0engineering numbers left to the language model. Every calculation is deterministic code, and a licensed engineer signs off.
Challenge
Every window and door permit needs wind-load calculations, a schedule of openings, drawings and a current Miami-Dade product approval for each product. It was all assembled by hand, and approvals that expire or get superseded went unchecked. A stale approval means a rejected permit.
What we built
A pipeline that takes a job in (order, floor plan, property photos) and reads it with a vision model. It runs the wind-load maths as code matched to the engineer's own workbook, fetches product approvals live and highlights the ratings that apply, then generates the schedule and a CAD drawing. A case library of past jobs, retrieved by structured features rather than text similarity, keeps new work consistent with how the firm has always done it.
Outcome
Delivered in milestones, each accepted by the client: intake to drawing, then floor plans and the case library, then live product-approval retrieval. The engineer reviews what the system read before anything is generated, and his word overrides it.
