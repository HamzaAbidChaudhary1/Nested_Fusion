Services

Four ways we take work off your plate.

Every engagement starts with the work your team does by hand today. We pick the right tool for it — an agent, a voice line, a workflow or an app — and build it to run unattended.

01AI agents 02Voice agents 03Workflow automation 04AI apps & tools

01

AI agents

Agents that read, reason and act across your tools — researching, triaging, drafting and updating records. Consequential actions wait for a person to approve them.

  • LangChain
  • LangGraph
  • OpenAI
  • Gemini
  • Tavily

Good for

  • Researching leads, competitors or markets and summarising what matters
  • Triaging inbound email and tickets, with drafted replies for approval
  • Reading documents and filling the right fields in your systems
  • Weekly reports compiled from several tools without anyone copying and pasting

You get

  • Agents running in your own accounts
  • Guardrails and human approval on anything consequential
  • Run logs, so you can see what the agent did and why
  • Plain-English documentation and a handover session

02

Voice agents

Phone agents that pick up every call, answer common questions, qualify the caller and book the appointment — then log a summary in your CRM.

  • Vapi
  • Calendars
  • CRM sync

Good for

  • Answering inbound calls after hours and at busy times
  • Qualifying callers and booking straight into your calendar
  • Outbound reminders, confirmations and follow-ups
  • Capturing every call as a transcript and summary

You get

  • A voice agent on a new number or your existing line
  • Call scripts and answers tuned to your business
  • Hand-off to a human when the caller needs one
  • Transcripts and summaries logged where your team works

03

Workflow automation

Reliable pipelines between the apps you already use. Built with error handling, retries and alerts, so a failure gets noticed instead of quietly losing data.

  • n8n
  • Make.com
  • GoHighLevel
  • Zapier
  • Webhooks

Good for

  • Client onboarding: forms, documents, CRM records and welcome emails
  • Lead routing and keeping your CRM clean
  • Order, invoice and fulfilment flows across platforms
  • Scheduled reporting and data syncs

You get

  • Workflows in your own n8n, Make.com or GoHighLevel account
  • Error handling, retries and failure alerts
  • A self-hosted n8n option when you want full control
  • Documentation for every flow

04

AI apps & internal tools

When a workflow needs a front end, we build one: copilots on your own documents, client portals, intake forms and dashboards — on a modern, maintainable stack.

  • Next.js
  • Supabase
  • FastAPI
  • TypeScript
  • Python

Good for

  • Internal copilots that answer from your documents and data
  • Client portals and multi-step intake forms
  • Operations dashboards in one place
  • Admin tools that sit on top of your automations

You get

  • A deployed web app with sign-in
  • Source code in your own repository
  • Integrations with your existing tools
  • Docs and a handover for your team or developers
