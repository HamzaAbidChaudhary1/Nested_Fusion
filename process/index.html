How it works

From messy process to running agent.

A short, structured path. You see working software on your own data early, and nothing goes live without your sign-off.

  1. 01 · Map

    Find the hours

    We sit with the people doing the work, trace each process end to end, and rank what to automate by time saved and risk.

    What happens

    • Short sessions with the people who do the work
    • Each process traced end to end, across every tool it touches
    • A ranked list of what to automate first

    You get

    • An automation plan in plain English
    • A clear recommendation on what not to automate

  2. 02 · Prototype

    Prove it on real data

    A working agent on your actual tools and data — not a slide deck — so you can judge the output before committing further.

    What happens

    • We connect to your tools with the minimum access needed
    • The agent runs on real examples from your business
    • You review its output with us and tell us what's off

    You get

    • A working prototype you can try yourself
    • A clear go / adjust / stop decision

  3. 03 · Deploy

    Ship it to production

    We harden the prototype for real use — guardrails, logging, approvals and fallbacks — and roll it out with your team.

    What happens

    • Edge cases handled, errors caught and alerted
    • Human approval added where the stakes are high
    • Rollout with the people who'll use it

    You get

    • A production agent running in your accounts
    • Documentation and a handover session

  4. 04 · Operate

    Keep it sharp

    We monitor runs, fix edge cases and extend what works — or hand it over fully documented for your team to own.

    What happens

    • Run logs reviewed, failures fixed
    • Prompts and rules improved as your business changes
    • The next process on the list gets started

    You get

    • An agent that keeps getting better
    • The option to take it fully in-house

From your side

What we need from you.

One process owner

Someone who knows the work today and can say what “good” looks like.

Access to the tools

Scoped access to the apps the process touches — only what the agent actually needs.

A little time each week

A short check-in to review output and make decisions, so the build never stalls.

Safeguards

Built in, not bolted on.

Human approval

Anything consequential — sending, paying, deleting — can wait for a person to approve it.

Run logs

Every run records what the agent saw, decided and did.

Fallbacks & alerts

When something fails, it retries, falls back, and tells someone.

Least access

Agents get only the permissions their job requires.
