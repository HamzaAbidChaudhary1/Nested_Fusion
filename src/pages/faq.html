Getting started

What kind of work is a good fit for an AI agent?

Repetitive, rules-heavy work that touches several tools: qualifying enquiries, answering routine calls, compiling reports, reconciling records, onboarding clients. If someone on your team says “I do this every week,” it's usually a candidate.

Do we need to change our software?

Rarely. We build on the tools you already use — your CRM, inbox, spreadsheets, e-commerce platform and phone line — and connect them, rather than asking your team to move.

How long until something is live?

It depends on scope, but we aim to put a working prototype on your real data early in the engagement, then harden it for production. You'll get a clear timeline after the discovery call.

What do you need from our team?

One person who knows the process, scoped access to the tools involved, and a short check-in each week to review output and make decisions.

Accuracy & data

How do you keep AI output accurate and safe?

Guardrails, structured outputs, validation steps and human approval for anything consequential. Agents log what they did and why, so your team can review and correct.

What access do you need to our systems?

Only what the agent needs to do its job. We work inside your own accounts wherever possible, so your data stays where it already lives.

What happens when the agent gets something wrong?

It's designed for that. Failures are caught and alerted, high-stakes steps wait for approval, and run logs make it easy to see what happened and fix it.

Ownership & cost

Who owns what you build?

You do. Workflows, code, prompts and documentation sit in your accounts from the start.

How is pricing worked out?

We scope each project after the discovery call, based on what's being automated and how it needs to run, and give you a clear proposal before any build starts.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. We can monitor and improve what we've built, add new automations over time, or hand everything over to your team with full documentation.

Do you work outside the US and Australia?

Yes. We mainly work with teams in the US and Australia, fully remotely, with working hours that overlap yours.
